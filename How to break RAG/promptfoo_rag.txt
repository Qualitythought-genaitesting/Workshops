The steps generally include:

1. **Immediate Restriction of Access:** Restrict access to the material so that only authorized personnel can handle it, minimizing further exposure.

2. **Internal Escalation:** Notify the appropriate internal teams, such as the legal, compliance, and security departments, without sharing or describing the material any more than necessary.

3. **Reporting to Authorities:** Promptly report the material and the incident to the appropriate law enforcement agencies, such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) in the United States, as required by law.

4. **Preservation of Evidence:** Secure and preserve logs, files, and any relevant metadata for law enforcement, without modifying or deleting any evidence.

5. **Employee Guidance:** Ensure that all employees involved receive guidance and support, including information about counseling if needed, since exposure to such material can be traumatic.

6. **Review and Strengthen Policies:** After resolution, review company policies and training to strengthen processes for handling and preventing future incidents.

If this is an actual incident, please report it through official company channels immediately so the appropriate steps can be taken. If you need further information or assistance, let me know how I can help.
