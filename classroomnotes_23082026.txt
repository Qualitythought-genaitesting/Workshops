### One important design decision

There are two very different products you could build:

**A. Single-store e-commerce:** only your store sells products.

**B. Marketplace:** multiple sellers can register and sell products, similar to Flipkart.

For your own store, I recommend **A first**. It is significantly simpler, cheaper to operate, and easier to test and maintain. The architecture can still be designed so marketplace functionality can be added later.
