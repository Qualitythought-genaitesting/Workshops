# Bali — Destination Guide
last_updated: 2024-08-05

## Best Time to Visit
The best time to visit Bali is during the dry season, from April to October, when the weather is sunny with low humidity.
The wet season runs from November to March and brings frequent rain.

## Attractions
Popular attractions include Ubud's rice terraces, Uluwatu Temple, Seminyak beaches and Mount Batur for sunrise hikes.

## Safety
Bali is generally safe for tourists. Take normal precautions with valuables and use licensed taxis.
