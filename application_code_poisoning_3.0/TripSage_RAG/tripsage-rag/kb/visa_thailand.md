# Thailand — Visa and Entry Requirements for Indian Passport Holders
last_updated: 2024-09-20

## Visa on Arrival
Indian passport holders can obtain a visa on arrival in Thailand for tourism for a stay of up to 15 days.
The visa on arrival fee is 2000 Thai Baht and requires a passport valid for at least 6 months, a return ticket and proof of funds.

## Longer Stays
For stays longer than 15 days, Indian travellers should apply for a tourist visa at a Thai embassy or consulate before travelling.

## Notes
Entry rules change frequently. Always confirm the current requirement with the official Thai embassy before you travel.
