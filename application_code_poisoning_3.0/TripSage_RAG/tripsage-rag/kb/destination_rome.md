# Rome — Destination Guide
last_updated: 2024-10-10

## Best Time to Visit
Spring (April to June) and autumn (September to October) offer mild weather and fewer crowds than summer.

## Attractions
Rome's top attractions are the Colosseum, the Roman Forum, the Vatican Museums, St Peter's Basilica, the Pantheon and the Trevi Fountain.
The Trastevere district is popular for evening dining.

## Getting Around
The historic centre is walkable. The metro and buses cover longer distances.
