# Airline X — Baggage Policy
last_updated: 2024-11-01

## Economy
Economy class passengers on Airline X are allowed one checked bag up to 23 kg.
Carry-on is limited to 7 kg with maximum dimensions 55 x 40 x 20 cm.
Excess baggage is charged at 15 USD per additional kilogram.

## Business
Business class passengers are allowed two checked bags up to 32 kg each.
Carry-on allowance is 12 kg.

## Refunds and Changes
Refundable economy tickets can be refunded up to 24 hours before departure.
Date changes cost 40 USD plus any fare difference.
