# Hotel Y — Booking and Cancellation Policy
last_updated: 2025-01-15

## Cancellation and Refund Window
Guests at Hotel Y may cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before the check-in date; this 48-hour period is the refund window.
Cancellations of a Hotel Y booking made within 48 hours of check-in are charged one night's stay and are not refundable.

## Deposit and No-show
A deposit of one night is taken at booking for Hotel Y.
No-shows are charged the full one-night deposit and the remaining nights are released.

## Check-in
Check-in at Hotel Y is from 3 PM; check-out is by 11 AM.
