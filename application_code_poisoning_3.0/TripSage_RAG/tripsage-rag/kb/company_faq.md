# TripSage — Company Travel FAQ
last_updated: 2025-02-01

## Booking
You can book flights and hotels through the TripSage booking portal. A confirmation email is sent after payment.

## Support
For help with an existing booking, contact support through the Help Centre in your account. TripSage answers general travel questions from its knowledge base.

## Refunds
Refund eligibility depends on the airline or hotel policy for your booking. See the relevant policy for exact windows.
