# Barcelona — Destination Guide
last_updated: 2024-10-12

## Best Time to Visit
May, June and September are the best months to visit Barcelona, with warm weather and lighter crowds than peak summer.

## Attractions
Barcelona's must-see sights include the Sagrada Familia, Park Guell, Casa Batllo, the Gothic Quarter and La Rambla.
Barceloneta beach is popular in summer.
